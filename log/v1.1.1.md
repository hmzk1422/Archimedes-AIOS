## Update Logs
- Llama model faces problem of over-refusal. It was triggered by meta's internal safety mechanism designed to be extremely cautious against financial fraud. That's why it kept saying 'i cannot help with financial fraud'
- Llama model lacks Chain-Of-Thought pre-training where Llama is trained to predict next word in sequence but gemini is trained with advanced-reasoning pre-testing. Llama didn't care about the logic (for example, clearly stating its 100 million IDR which is below min sum assured 200 million IDR is still possible)
- Llama model is binary. If a word triggered safety flag, conversation stops. Gemini has better context-aware safety filtering and sematic intent of conversation. Gemini also has native multimodal integration where internal representations handles structured information very well.
