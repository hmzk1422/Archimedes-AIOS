# Legacy Pro summary
- Insurance period: Protects insured up to age 100
- Premium payout terms: 5, 10, 15 years
- Available Currencies: IDR or USD
- Minimum basic premium: IDR 200,000,000 or USD 20,000
- Insured's Age: for 5-10 year payout term, 1month - 70 years old. For 15 year payout term, 1 month - 59 years old
- Policyholder's Age: At least 18 years old

# Core Benefit
- Death benefit: If insured passes away in coverage period, Allianz pays 100% of Sum Assured
- If insured passes away above equal age 75, sum assured increase by 50% (if using e-policy). Otherwise, 25%.'
- Basic premium waiver: Allianz will waive future basic insurance premium if the insured is diagnosed with one of 77 covered critical illness during payment period.
