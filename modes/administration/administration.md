# How to Top Up Premium?
Top up premium if unit link balance is underwhelming
- Open Allianz eAZy Connect. Log in to your account. Click Unit Link, Top Up Premium, insert amount in IDR (100,000 to 100,000,000 IDR max).
- Alternative method: fill Allianz Top Up Premium Proposal Form then send it to Allianz Office.

# How to Appeal Claim
For more reference, refer to each policy. General requirements:
- Identification Card
- Doctor's confirmation letter with signature
- Original policy copy
- Family card
- Bank account number
Wait +-7 days for email confirmation. If email hasn't been received yet, contact Allianz.
